YOUR LOGO FOR ARCKAD-D
======================
1. Save your logo here as:   branding/logo.png
   - square (if not, it's centred on a transparent square)
   - at least 512 x 512 pixels (1024 x 1024 is ideal)
   - transparent background (PNG)
2. Commit and push. The automatic build makes every icon from it:
   Windows program and installer, Android launcher, Ubuntu menu, and the logo inside the app.

To preview the icons on your computer first:  python3 branding/make-icons.py
(needs Pillow:  sudo apt install python3-pil)

Without branding/logo.png the built-in logo is used.
